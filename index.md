## About Me

I am a **Joint PhD Student** at **[Shanghai AI Lab](https://www.shlab.org.cn/)** and **[HIT-SCIR](https://ir.hit.edu.cn/)**, advised by **[Yu Cheng](https://ych133.github.io/)** and **[Weinan Zhang](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/zhangweinan)**.

My research interests lie in **AI agents** and **generative models**. My work explores memory and planning in long-horizon agents, as well as transferring agent experience into generative models. I am interested in how agents can learn from experience and how agents and generative models can improve together.

## Research Interests

- **AI Agents:** Memory, planning, and learning from experience for long-horizon tasks.
- **Generative Models:** Transferring agent experience into generative models and exploring their co-evolution.
